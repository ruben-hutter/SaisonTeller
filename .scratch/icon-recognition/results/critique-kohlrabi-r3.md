# kohlrabi (round 3)

**1. What it reads as now:**
- Reads as a **turnip/radish or alien UFO plant** — the big pale sphere with a tiny tail reads "turnip," and the three stalks sprouting from the *top* read as sprouts, a sprouted onion, or a cartoon alien.
- Kohlrabi's key trait — the bulb sitting **on top of the ground with leaves sprouting from its sides/stem** — is missing. Real kohlrabi leaves grow from a central stem on the bulb's shoulder, not on long tentacle-like stalks radiating outward.
- The wavy lines read as generic "texture" or a face, adding nothing.

**2. Top 3 fixes:**
- **Rebuild the silhouette:** bulb should be a slightly flattened sphere *with a thick cut stem stub on top*, not a perfect ball with a root tail. Kill the tail — that's turnip language.
- **Replace the tentacles with leaf stems:** 2–3 short, thick petioles emerging from the bulb's top-center/shoulder, each ending in a broad **leaf blade** (big rounded/oval leaves), not floating ellipses on wires.
- **Use kohlrabi colors:** pale green bulb (not cream/pale yellow — that says turnip) with slightly darker green leaves; drop or sharpen the wavy lines into subtle vertical shading strokes only.

**3. Canonical view:**
- **Front view, whole bulb sitting upright**, slightly squat/flattened sphere in pale green, a short cut stem nub on top, and 2–3 large round leaves on short stalks fanning up from the top — like a green cabbage-hearted globe with a leaf crown. Silhouette must survive at 32px: *globe + stem nub + leaf blades*, nothing else.
