# shallot (round 17)

**1. WHAT IT READS AS NOW:** Beetroot / red onion / purple garlic bulb. The tall egg shapes with vertical stripes read as beet slices or red onion; the green sprouts read as scallion/garlic greens; nothing signals "shallot cluster" specifically — bulbs are too elongated and too saturated purple.

**2. TOP 3 FIXES:**
- **Shorten & fatten the bulbs:** shallots are squat ovals/teardrops, wider than tall-tapered — currently they look like beets.
- **Change skin color to copper/rose-tan** (magenta-brown or papery red-gold) with papery sheen lines, not deep magenta — that purple is beet territory.
- **Show the cluster structure:** 2–3 distinct lobes with visible dividing lines/cloves and a root tuft on one side, stems trimmed short (shallots' tell vs. scallions).

**3. CANONICAL VIEW:** Whole-bulb side view — a squat coppery-purple teardrop with a short trimmed green stub on top, faint vertical papery lines, and small root beard at the base; optionally 2 lobes touching to suggest the multi-clove cluster.
