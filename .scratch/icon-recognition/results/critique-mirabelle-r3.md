# mirabelle (round 3)

**1. WHAT IT READS AS NOW**
- Two plain yellow/gold circles — reads as generic **balls, coins, or cookies**, not fruit.
- The tiny curved red mark and faint arcs are unreadable; they don't suggest plum flesh or shine.
- The green stem is too small and sprouts from only one sphere — the other circle looks like a loose ball.
- No leaf-fruit logic, no dimple, no crease: nothing anchors it as any fruit, let alone a plum.

**2. TOP 3 FIXES**
- **Add a vertical crease/dimple line** on each fruit (the signature plum groove) — instantly says "stone fruit."
- **Unify the silhouette**: overlap the two fruits clearly and give *both* a stem, or one stem with a small leaf; right now they're disconnected circles.
- **Shift the color away from pure gold**: use a warmer yellow with an orange/red blush patch and a white specular highlight — flat gold reads as coin/ball.

**3. CANONICAL VIEW**
- **Single round yellow plum, slight side/three-quarter view**, with a visible **creased seam line**, a **dimple at top where a short stem + single green leaf attach**, subtle blush and one highlight dot. Two fruits are fine only if overlapping with clear depth (one behind, one in front, back one darker).
