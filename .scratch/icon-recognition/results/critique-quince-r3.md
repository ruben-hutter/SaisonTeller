# quince (round 3)

**1. WHAT IT READS AS NOW**
- It reads as a generic **lemon** (or orange): plain yellow circle + stem.
- Causes: perfect sphere silhouette, saturated lemon-yellow, no dimples/pebbling, no asymmetric bulge, leaf too generic.

**2. TOP 3 FIXES**
- **Shape:** Kill the circle — make it slightly squat and irregular with a wide, bumpy bottom and a pronounced **depRESSED top/crown** (quinces have a lobed, apple-like waist near the stem).
- **Color:** Shift to muted **golden-yellow with a warm/orange-green blush** — not bright lemon yellow. Add fuzzy-texture cue (subtle speckle or matte outline) since quinces are fuzzy.
- **Stem/crown:** Thicker, woody stem set in a visible **dimpled cavity**, leaf heavier and darker green.

**3. CANONICAL VIEW**
- Slightly pear/apple-hybrid silhouette shown in **¾ or straight-on side view**: squat round body, distinct indented top with thick stem + one leaf, matte golden-yellow with an orange blush and fuzzy speckle texture.
