# radicchio (round 16)

**1. WHAT IT READS AS NOW:** A red balloon or Christmas ornament — the perfect circle, small tail at bottom, and no leaf structure at all scream "balloon." The cream branching veins read as reindeer antlers or a decorative monogram, not leaf veins. Zero radicchio cues: radicchio needs layered/overlapping leaf edges and a bulbous, ribbed head shape.

**2. TOP 3 FIXES:**
- **Kill the perfect circle + tail.** Replace with a slightly flattened, bulbous teardrop/oval and remove the bottom nub entirely (it's the #1 "balloon" trigger).
- **Add leaf separation.** Show overlapping leaf tips at the top (a few pointed cream/magenta lobes poking over the silhouette) and visible leaf edges/lines around the perimeter — radicchio is defined by its layered wrap.
- **Anchor the veins in a white heart.** Put a large white/cream central core (the classic cut-radichio heart) with magenta veins radiating from it, instead of floating antler-like branches on flat red.

**3. CANONICAL VIEW:** Whole head from a ¾/front angle: bulbous oval with pointed leaf tips at the top, one or two visible outer leaf outlines, and a white core with thick magenta veins — like a cross between an onion and a cabbage. Alternatively, the *halved head* (white center, magenta veins, red rim) is instantly readable and a strong fallback.
