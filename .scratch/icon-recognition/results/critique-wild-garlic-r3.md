# wild-garlic (round 3)

**1. WHAT IT READS AS NOW:**
- Reads as a **cactus with a white flower**, or a **golf ball on a tee**, or a **lollipop/tree toy**.
- Killers: the vertical segmented "stem" shape looks like a saguaro cactus; the white circle with holes reads as a golf ball/lollipop, not garlic. Wild garlic's actual garlic **bulb is absent** — the white blob floats at the top like a flower head.
- Real wild garlic icons show **two to three broad, lily-of-the-valley-like pointed leaves** on a thin stem, sometimes a small elongated white bulb at the base.

**2. TOP 3 FIXES:**
- **Delete the white circle on top** (it's a golf ball/lollipop cue) and replace with **2–3 wide, arching, lance-shaped leaves** that taper to sharp points — the leaf silhouette is the entire identity of ramsons.
- **Extend the thin green stem downward and end it in a small pointed white/cream bulb** (elongated teardrop, like a scallion bulb) so it reads as a garlic-family plant.
- **Reduce the number of "fingers"**: the 5 identical vertical pods must become 2–3 fanning, curved leaves with smooth solid green fill and a lighter center vein — no cactus ribbing/segment lines.

**3. CANONICAL VIEW:**
- **Front view, straight-on**: a short white bulb at the bottom, one thin stem, fanning out into **three broad pointed green leaves (center upright, two arching outward)** — the classic ramsons "hand of leaves" silhouette. That's it: bulb + stem + 3 pointed leaves, nothing else.
