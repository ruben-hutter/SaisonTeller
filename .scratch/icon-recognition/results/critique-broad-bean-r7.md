# broad-bean (round 7)

**1. What it reads as now:**
- It reads as a **pea pod** (or an open mouth/eyeball/hockey puck with teeth). The fully rounded outer oval + four identical circles + a stem nub = generic pea pod.
- The dark bars read as "seams/eyes," not hilums. No bean-specific cues survive the simplification.
- The flat symmetric oval kills the fava bean's signature feature.

**2. Top 3 fixes:**
- **Show the hilum.** A fava bean's identity = the distinct black/brown oval scar (hilum) on the edge of each bean. Make it a clearly placed elongated black mark on the bean's edge, not a centered "mouth" stripe. This is the #1 differentiator from peas, which have no hilum.
- **Make beans kidney/oval-uneven, not circles.** Fava beans are large, flat, slightly kidney-shaped and asymmetric. Vary bean size/tilt slightly; chunky > round.
- **Give the pod character.** Use a plump, slightly curved pod with a bulging silhouette (pinched ends), fuzzy/velvet interior hint (lighter inner fill), and a stubby curved stem — not a perfect ellipse. Or drop the pod entirely.

**3. Canonical view:**
- **A single shelled fava bean, 3/4 or side view:** big flat kidney-ish oval in green, with a prominent dark brown/black hilum line on one edge. Optionally a split pod with 2–3 chunky beans showing hilums. One bean + hilum = instantly "fava," not pea.
