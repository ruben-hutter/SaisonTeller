# salsify (round 3)

**1. What it reads as now:**
- A white radish/daikon or parsnip with green sprouts — the pale cream body and green leafy top scream "daikon."
- The thin tan lines read as ribs/seams, suggesting a squash or gourd.
- The odd dark triangular wedge at bottom looks like a blemish or shadow, not black skin.
- Legs: the small side appendages read as insect or animal limbs, making it look like a cartoon creature.
- Overall misread: **daikon radish (or turnip), possibly a bug.**

**2. Top 3 fixes:**
- **Make the root black/dark brown.** Black salsify is literally named for its near-black skin — the pale body is the #1 failure. One solid dark elongated shape.
- **Kill the legs and the dark wedge.** Nothing grows sideways off a salsify root; remove all side appendages and the blemish triangle.
- **Replace sprout-cluster with long straight green tops.** Salsify leaves are grass-like blades rising from the root tip, not a broccoli-like clump of blobs. Add 3–5 thin upright green blades.

**3. Canonical view:**
- **Vertical, slightly tapered dark (near-black) cylinder with one end cut/peeled showing pale cream flesh**, topped with a few long thin green blades. Optionally show one peeled section — the black-root/white-flesh contrast is salsify's single most identifying trait.
