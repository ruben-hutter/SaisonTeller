# kale (round 9)

**1. WHAT IT READS AS NOW**
- Reads as generic lettuce, celery, or a head of broccoli — not kale.
- Why: smooth rounded lobes + tidy fan of stems = lettuce/celery. Kale's defining trait, the frilly ruffled leaf edges, is only a faint scallop.
- No dark vein texture, no crinkled silhouette; it's too symmetrical and too "clean."

**2. TOP 3 FIXES**
- **Ruff up the silhouette:** make the leaf edges deeply scalloped/frilled — irregular bumps and curling lobes, not neat semicircles. The outline should look crinkly at a glance.
- **Break the fan symmetry:** overlap leaves at varied angles and lengths with visible dark center ribs/veins on each leaf; kale is a loose bouquet, not a comb.
- **Boost color contrast:** darker green leaves with lighter, pronounced midribs so the blistered/curly leaf texture reads, instead of flat two-tone blobs.

**3. CANONICAL VIEW**
- Front view of a loose bunch: 3–5 upright leaves with heavily ruffled, irregular edges, thick pale central stalks fanning from a short base stub — silhouette first (frilly outline), color second.
