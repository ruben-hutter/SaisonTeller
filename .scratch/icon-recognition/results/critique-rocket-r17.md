# rocket (round 17)

**1. WHAT IT READS AS NOW**
- Reads as a generic houseplant/succulent or mimosa-style flower sprig — not an edible leafy green.
- The paired, rounded oval leaves on stiff upright stems look like *eucalyptus* or *pennywort*, not arugula.
- The yellow-white flower cluster on top is the biggest offender: arugula icons almost never feature prominent blooms; flowers dominate the silhouette and scream "flowering herb."
- Missing arugula's defining traits: elongated **lobed/serrated leaves** and a slender pale stem.

**2. TOP 3 FIXES**
- **Delete the flowers entirely.** Arugula's identity is its leaves; the blossom cluster steals the read.
- **Redraw the leaves as elongated, lobed shapes** — long narrow leaves with 3–5 rounded lobes along each side, tapering to a tip. This serrated outline IS arugula.
- **Vary the composition:** 3–5 loose stems fanning out with leaves at different angles, rather than two stiff mirrored stems with evenly stacked symmetrical leaves (that symmetry is what makes it read as eucalyptus).

**3. CANONICAL VIEW**
- Straight-on **bunch view**: several slender light-green stems fanning upward, each carrying 4–6 dark-green elongated leaves with lobed/serrated edges angled alternately up the stem, stems converging at the base. No flowers, no pot, no symmetry.
