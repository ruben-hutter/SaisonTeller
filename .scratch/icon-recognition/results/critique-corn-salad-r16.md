# corn-salad (round 16)

**1. WHAT IT READS AS NOW:**
- Scattered spade/heart-shaped blobs with no structure — reads as **fallen leaves, scattered petals, or confetti**, possibly "mint leaves spilled."
- The detached pieces float with gaps; there's no plant logic (no rosette, no stems, no clustering), so the brain can't assemble "salad green."
- Two-tone greens on random fragments look like a broken/explosion animation frame, not food.

**2. TOP 3 FIXES:**
- **Unify into a tight rosette:** lamb's lettuce is defined by its low, fan-shaped rosette of spoon-shaped leaves — overlap the leaves around a center point instead of scattering them.
- **Give leaves a real silhouette:** long, rounded *spoon/tongue* shapes with tiny stem hints at the base, not heart/spade tips. Remove the notched tops.
- **Add a grounding anchor:** a small cluster or a bunch (leaves tied at stems) so it reads as "harvested greens," not debris. Drop the loose fragments entirely.

**3. CANONICAL VIEW:**
- **Top-down rosette:** 6–8 rounded spatulate leaves radiating from a central point, slightly overlapping, single green fill with darker inner leaves — essentially a small, tight bouquet viewed from above. That's the instantly recognizable mâche icon.
