# cabbage-savoy (round 3)

**1. WHAT IT READS AS NOW**
- Reads as an abstract leaf/paisley or a lopsided fleur-de-lis — not a cabbage.
- Central blob has two overlapping organic shapes with stitch-like marks; they look like seams or sutures, not leaf veins.
- Two side "leaves" read as flat crescents/petals, disconnected from the head — so no "round cabbage" silhouette forms.

**2. TOP 3 FIXES**
- **Unify the silhouette:** make the whole thing read as one tight, round sphere (slightly squat circle/oval). Right now it's vertically elongated and lumpy — savoy is round.
- **Real savoy detail:** replace the stitch marks with concentric wavy/ruffled leaf layers wrapping the sphere — curved bands following the ball's contour, with bumpy scalloped edges. That crinkled wrapping is THE savoy cue.
- **Kill the detached side crescents or attach them:** outer leaves should hug and overlap the head's edge as cupped, ruffled leaves, not float beside it like flower petals.

**3. CANONICAL VIEW**
- A whole cabbage head seen from the front/top: a solid round (slightly wide) circle in mid-green, wrapped by 3–4 overlapping wavy leaf bands in alternating greens with scalloped/ruffled outer edges and a small stem nub at the bottom. Round, layered, crinkled — never elongated or split into floating parts.
