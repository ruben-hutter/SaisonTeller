# raspberry (round 9)

**1. WHAT IT READS AS NOW**
- Reads as a **bunch of grapes / wine grapes**. The stacked circles hanging from a green stem with leaves is the universal grape-clipart formula.
- The uniform circle sizes and dark outlines amplify the "grape cluster" reading — raspberries are NOT a hanging cluster of round balls.
- Green leaves fanning out from the top stem is a grape/stemmed-fruit cue, not a raspberry cue.

**2. TOP 3 FIXES**
- **Use a single bumpy blob, not circles:** draw one rounded, slightly cone/heart-shaped mass with scalloped bumps on the outline (drupelets), or tightly overlapping small circles forming one solid body — not distinct separated balls.
- **Add the signature hollow core / tapered silhouette:** wider at top, narrowing to a rounded point at the bottom, with a tiny indentation or no stem at the tip (raspberries detach from the plant).
- **Kill the grape stem + leaves setup:** replace the long vertical stem and side leaves with a small green calyx/star cap peeking from the top only. Drop the dark outlines or keep them minimal — berries read better clean.

**3. CANONICAL VIEW**
Slight 3/4 or straight-on view of a **single berry**: teardrop body made of interlocking bumps, subtle drupelet highlights (a few pale dots), green star-shaped calyx on top, **no stem, no leaves fanning out**. One berry is instantly recognizable; clusters read as grapes.
