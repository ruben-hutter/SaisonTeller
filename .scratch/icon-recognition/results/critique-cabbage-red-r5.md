# cabbage-red (round 5)

**1. WHAT IT READS AS NOW:** A flower (dahlia/aster) or a Ferris wheel. The radial symmetry, rounded petals, and white spokes diverging from a dark hub are classic flower iconography. Nothing says "leafy vegetable" — no layered leaf texture, no veins, no stem structure.

**2. TOP 3 FIXES:**
- **Kill the radial "petals + hub" layout.** Red cabbage reads as overlapping layered leaves, not a radial bloom. Use concentric arcs/scalloped layers with visible leaf veins.
- **Add veins and leaf edges.** Curved pale lines inside leaf shapes, plus slightly wavy/irregular outer silhouette instead of perfect scalloped circles.
- **Replace the hub with a compact inner head** (small tight ball of layered leaves) peeking out from outer wrapping leaves — side/¾ view, not top-down mandala view.

**3. CANONICAL VIEW:** A **¾ or side view bulb**: big outer leaves wrapping around left/right with visible veins, converging to a tight round center, on a short stubby stem. Round overall silhouette, purple gradient (light lavender center → deep purple outer leaves). Think "onion icon" structure but with leaf veins and purple palette.
