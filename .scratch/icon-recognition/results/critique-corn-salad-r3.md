# corn-salad (round 3)

**1. WHAT IT READS AS NOW:**
- Reads as a generic leafy garnish, spinach/salad leaves, or even a green ribbon/bow.
- The fan of rounded petals + a central dot reads like a lotus or flower icon.
- The odd hook/tendril at top looks like a misplaced stem or a clip handle — dominant shape, but meaningless here.
- No rosette structure: lamb's lettuce's defining trait is a tight bunch of small spoon-shaped leaves from one base.

**2. TOP 3 FIXES:**
- **Kill the tendril loop.** It's the biggest confusion source. Replace with a few short stems fanning from the base.
- **Make leaves small, numerous, and spoon/oval-shaped** (5–8 narrow rounded leaves), not 5 broad fan petals. Narrow = darker green, delicate character.
- **Compose as a low rosette/clump** viewed at 3/4: leaves radiating up-and-out from a single point, slightly uneven heights, maybe one lighter leaf for depth. Ditch the central dot circle.

**3. CANONICAL VIEW:**
- Slight 3/4 overhead view of a **rosette bunch**: 6–9 small spatulate leaves radiating from a short stem base, mid-green (#6FA843-ish) with 1–2 lighter highlights, compact and tuft-like — like a tiny bouquet, not a fan.
