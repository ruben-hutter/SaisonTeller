# sage (round 3)

**1. WHAT IT READS AS NOW:** A bug/moth. The two large symmetrical oval "wings" with dots dominate, and the vertical bud reads as a body/antennae — it reads as an insect or butterfly more than sage.

**2. TOP 3 FIXES:**
- Kill the wing symmetry: make leaves elongated, lanceolate, and slightly asymmetric/overlapping (sage leaves are long and pointed, not round).
- Remove the dots — stippling reads as insect wing spots; if texture is needed, use a faint central vein line instead.
- Shrink or remove the upright bud; emphasize a visible stem with 2–3 pointed leaves attached at staggered heights.

**3. CANONICAL VIEW:** A small sprig seen straight-on: a short green stem with 2–3 pairs of narrow, pointed, gray-green leaves (soft matte sage color, slightly dusty tone), maybe one subtle center vein per leaf. No round leaves, no dots, no vertical bud.
