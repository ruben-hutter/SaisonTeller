# cabbage-red (round 8)

**1. WHAT IT READS AS NOW:**
- Reads as a purple apple/plum/ornament — round purple body, stem on top, leaf, and a "stem dot" at the bottom.
- The three white curved stripes read as highlights or mouth/face lines, not layered cabbage leaves.
- Green lumps on the sides read as leaves/wrappers, reinforcing "fruit with leaves."
- No layering, veining, or cabbage "rosette" structure at all.

**2. TOP 3 FIXES:**
- **Kill the top stem and the bottom dot.** Stems = fruit. A cabbage has no stem icon-cue.
- **Replace the three white stripes with concentric leaf layers:** draw overlapping curved bands/arcs (darker purple vein lines + alternating light/dark purple layers) wrapping around a visible core swirl — the cabbage "spiral" is THE identifier.
- **Fix the silhouette:** make it squat/wider-than-tall with a slightly flat top (cut stem face) and outer leaves flaring open at the sides — not a perfect circle.

**3. CANONICAL VIEW:**
Half or whole head viewed slightly from above showing the **top-down spiral rosette** — concentric swirl of purple leaf layers with a small core dot in the center — optionally with 2–3 darker outer leaves flaring out. That swirl silhouette is unmistakably cabbage; a plain purple ball never will be.
