# broad-bean (round 3)

**1. What it reads as now:** A green jelly bean / candy / pea — the bean pod shape is too rounded and blob-like, and the three "seeds" are pale circles with weak green crescents that look like reflection highlights rather than beans. Misread: jelly bean / lime candy.

**2. Top 3 fixes:**
- **Add the pod crease:** a curved seam line running down the middle of the pod (the flattened edge of a broad bean) — this is the #1 identifier currently missing.
- **Push the seeds:** make beans flat, dark-green ovals with a distinct hilum line (dark edge/strip), filling more of the pod — not pale circles floating inside.
- **Fix the silhouette:** broaden the pod into a slightly kidney/lens shape with a tapered stem tip at one end, instead of a symmetrical blob.

**3. Canonical view:** An open pod (side view, split along the crease) showing 2–4 flat, dark-green beans with visible hilum lines, pod slightly curved with a small stem tip — the "open pea pod but fat beans" formula.
