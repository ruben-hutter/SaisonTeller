# broad-bean (round 5)

**1. WHAT IT READS AS NOW:**
- Reads as a **pea pod (green pea)** or even an **avocado/chili pepper** — a curved green crescent with round dots inside.
- The stem curls like a **chili/wasabi stalk**, pushing toward "pepper."
- The bean "seeds" are too pea-like: circles with smile-shaped highlights = classic pea iconography.

**2. TOP 3 FIXES:**
- **Use 3–4 chunky oval/kidney-shaped beans**, tightly packed edge-to-edge filling the pod — not loose round peas with gaps. Fava beans are large, flat, and oblong.
- **Shorten and thicken the stem** — a stub, not a long curly vine hook. Curly stem = chili/tendril, not fava.
- **Show the pod open wider with a flat, angular silhouette** (fava pods are broad, flattish, slightly puffy with rounded bumps — not a smooth crescent). Add the characteristic **black "eye" (hilum) dot** on one bean — that's the fava's signature tell.

**3. CANONICAL VIEW:**
- **Open pod, three-quarter tilt (~45°), showing 3–4 large overlapping flat oval beans inside, one with a visible black hilum dot**, thick stub stem at top. That combination is unmistakably "broad bean" at icon size.
