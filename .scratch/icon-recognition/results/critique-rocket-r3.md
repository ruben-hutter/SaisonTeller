# rocket (round 3)

1. WHAT IT READS AS NOW:
- Reads as a **frog** (two rounded lobes with eye-like slits) or a generic plant/leaf sprout. The two side lobes + small marks read as frog eyes, not arugula.
- Arugula's defining features — long jagged/serrated lobes along a slender stem — are completely absent. Symmetry kills it.

2. TOP 3 FIXES:
- **Kill the symmetry and roundness**: replace the two fat mirrored lobes with a single stem bearing 4–6 **elongated, lobed leaves** — each leaf with a large end lobe and 2–3 deep rounded notches below it (lyre/pinnate shape).
- **Show arugula's silhouette in ONE leaf**: the leaf outline alone must say "arugula" — oak-leaf-like, irregular, pointing down along the stem, not a smooth oval.
- **Drop the "face" marks and central line-through-leaf layout**; use slight rotation/asymmetry so leaves hang off one stem, alternating sides.

3. CANONICAL VIEW:
- A slightly angled bunch/branch: thin light-green stem with 3–5 **deeply lobed, ragged-edged leaves** alternating along it, each leaf narrowing toward the stem and widening into a rounded tip. The jagged leaf outline — not green color — is what makes arugula legible.
