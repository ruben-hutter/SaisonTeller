# marjoram (round 3)

**1. WHAT IT READS AS NOW**
- Reads as a generic "leaf sprig" — most testers would say **rosemary or eucalyptus branch**, or just "plant/eco logo."
- Causes: alternating round blobs on a thin stem reads as *eucalyptus (silver dollar)*, not marjoram. Marjoram's identity = **small oval leaves in opposite PAIRS along the stem**, dense at the tip.
- The pale cream cluster at the top is ambiguous noise — reads as berries or a flower, making it look like a berry branch.
- Colors are desaturated pastel; no herb-green saturation, so it reads "decorative," not "culinary herb."

**2. TOP 3 FIXES**
- **Opposite paired leaves:** draw leaves in symmetric pairs (two per node) descending the stem, slightly overlapping — this is marjoram/oregano's signature. Kill the alternating zigzag.
- **Remove or shrink the top cluster** into a tight bud of tiny leaves at the tip (a compact "crown"), or replace with a few small white blossoms clearly attached to leaf axils — not a floating blob.
- **Boost green saturation** to a fresh medium herb-green with darker outlines/shadows for leaf separation; small ovate leaves with pointed tips, not perfect circles.

**3. CANONICAL VIEW**
- **Single upright stem, slight diagonal, with 3–4 opposite leaf pairs getting smaller toward the top, ending in a compact leafy bud tip.** Frontal flat view, one green tone + darker vein/shadow, small white flower dots at upper leaf joints. That paired-leaves sprig is the instantly recognizable marjoram silhouette.
