# chicory (round 3)

**1. WHAT IT READS AS NOW**
- Reads as an **archway/gateway, hair curl, or "n" shape** — not food at all.
- Killers: the big pale arch dominates everything; the two small stacked shapes below read as columns/pillars with random beige smudges (eyebrows?); the thin curved lines floating inside look like dangling straps or antennae. Nothing anchors it as a vegetable.

**2. TOP 3 FIXES**
- **Show tight, tapered torpedo shapes, not an arch.** Belgian endive = compact elongated bullet of overlapping leaves, tip narrow, base blunt/cut off. Draw 2–3 slightly fanned endives side by side.
- **Add the pale-yellow color coding.** Use a distinct **butter/cream-yellow** (not white-on-white) — the blanched tip vs. slightly greener base contrast is the #1 recognizability cue. Current palette is invisible on white.
- **Add the cut stem base** as a flat oval/ellipse at the bottom of each head — this instantly says "packed vegetable," not "archway."

**3. CANONICAL VIEW**
- **Upright, slightly fanned bundle of 2–3 pale-cream-yellow bullets**, tips leaning outward, with subtle overlapping leaf strokes running vertically and a flat cut base ellipse at the bottom — like chicory in a produce-bin illustration. Drop the arch entirely.
