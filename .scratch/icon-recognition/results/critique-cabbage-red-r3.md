# cabbage-red (round 3)

**1. WHAT IT READS AS NOW**
- It reads as a **purple scoop of ice cream / berry / blueberry**. The big circle with three curved highlight strokes = classic ice cream ball shading; the two side bumps look like scoops behind it, not leaves.
- No stem, no leaf veins, no layered leaf edges — nothing says "cabbage."

**2. TOP 3 FIXES**
- **Kill the sphere + swoosh highlights.** Those three curved strokes are the #1 culprit (ice cream/glossy ball shorthand). Replace with **leaf vein lines**: branching strokes or wavy horizontal contour lines that follow leaf layers.
- **Break the silhouette.** A cabbage is a tight rosette of overlapping leaves. Show **scalloped/wavy leaf edges** around the perimeter and 2–3 overlapping leaf arcs in front, not side blobs.
- **Add context cues**: a short **stem nub at the bottom** and, ideally, a couple of loose outer leaves splaying out — the universal "head of cabbage" read.

**3. CANONICAL VIEW**
- **3/4 top-down view of the whole head**: roundish bulb, wavy leaf-edge silhouette, visible concentric leaf layers on top with vein strokes radiating from a small center, purple with lighter lavender leaf separations. That's the unmistakable flat-icon cabbage.
