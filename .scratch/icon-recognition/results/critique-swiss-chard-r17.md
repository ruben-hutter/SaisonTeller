# swiss-chard (round 17)

**1. WHAT IT READS AS NOW**
- Reads as generic "plant/bush" or "trees cluster" — or at best generic leafy greens like kale/parsley.
- Killers: yellow stalks look like trunks/stems; leaves are three separate rounded blobs; central spine lines read like branches; no color contrast between stalk and leaf veins.

**2. TOP 3 FIXES**
- **Unify leaf + stalk:** make leaves broad, ovate, and overlapping into one bunch; drop the three-tree fan layout.
- **Use chard's signature colors:** bright magenta/red (or neon yellow) thick stalks and veins against deep green leaves — that contrast IS the identifier.
- **Thicken the veins so they read as fleshy midribs** (wide, stalk-colored channels running up into the leaf), not thin twig branches.

**3. CANONICAL VIEW**
Front view of one upright bunch: 3–5 broad crinkled green leaves fanning from a thick cut base, with fat red/magenta stalks and matching veins running up each leaf — silhouette closer to a bouquet of romaine than trees.
