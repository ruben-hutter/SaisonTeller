# cabbage-red (round 10)

**1. WHAT IT READS AS NOW:** A **plum / peach / eggplant**. The misread comes from:
- Big smooth purple sphere + oval leaf + stem = classic **plum icon formula**
- The three white curved lines read as a peach crease or glossy highlight, not leaf veins
- Green foliage blob behind reads as generic leaves, not cabbage wrapper leaves
- Perfectly circular silhouette = fruit, not vegetable

**2. TOP 3 FIXES:**
- **Break the circle:** show overlapping rounded leaf lobes with a scalloped/wavy outer silhouette — cabbage is defined by layered leaves, not a smooth ball
- **Use the cross-section:** half-cut view with the concentric leaf-layer spiral is the canonical cabbage cue; even side-view horizontal white arcs should be layered leaf edges, not 3 thin stripes
- **Recolor the leaves:** purple leaves with lighter purple/white vein ribs wrapping around the front, ditch the dark-green blob (green + purple round = plum/fig)

**3. CANONICAL VIEW:** Front view of a **whole head with 2–3 overlapping wavy leaves** (scalloped edges, visible leaf tips curling outward) in purple/violet, veins rendered as light ribbed lines — or the **halved head showing the layered spiral core**, which is instantly "cabbage" to any viewer.
