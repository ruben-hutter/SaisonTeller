# celeriac (round 17)

**1. WHAT IT READS AS NOW:**
- Reads as **coconut with palm sprouts** or a **pufferfish/potato with stems** — the round speckled tan blob dominates.
- The stalks are too long, straight, and evenly splayed — they look like plant stems, not cut celery tops.
- Missing the two key celeriac cues: **root hairs at the bottom are too faint/tiny**, and there's no **cut top / leafy stubble** look.
- Overall silhouette = sphere + sticks; nothing says "knobby root vegetable."

**2. TOP 3 FIXES:**
- **Kill the symmetry:** make the bulb irregular, lumpy, slightly squat — an off-round silhouette with visible bumps/creases. Celeriac is never a clean circle.
- **Shorten and roughen the stalks:** stubby, uneven cut stems (like harvested celery), not tall graceful fronds. Fewer leaflets, thicker stems.
- **Strengthen root details:** prominent rootlet tufts at the base, plus a banded/striped or heavily speckled skin texture in a duller, more brownish tan (current color is too "coconut husk").

**3. CANONICAL VIEW:**
- **Front view of the whole bulb, slightly squat and knobby**, with **4–5 short cut celery stems (2–3 leaves each)** sticking up from a pale cut crown, and a **spray of thin rootlets at the bottom**. Compress everything vertically so the bulb is ~60–70% of the icon.
