# oregano (round 3)

**1. WHAT IT READS AS NOW:** A bird/butterfly or abstract heart — two symmetric lobe clusters meeting in the middle read as wings, the brown stems as legs/antennae, and the floating dots as sparkle/mist. Nothing signals an herb sprig; it reads as symmetric wildlife icon, not produce.

**2. TOP 3 FIXES:**
- **Kill the symmetry & the central merge.** Two stems crossing like a wishbone is the #1 misread trigger. Show 2–3 parallel stems fanning from one base point (a bundled sprig bouquet) or a single stem.
- **Make leaves ovoid, pointed, and alternating along the stem** — attached in pairs up the stem with visible stem tips extending past the last leaf. Currently leaves are blob clusters detached from structure, reading as petals/wings.
- **Remove the floating dots entirely** (or reduce to 2–3 tiny leaves at stem tips). Dots above a symmetric shape = sparkle/scent cue that pushes it toward "butterfly/magic." Use 2 greens max (e.g., #6B8E3F leaves, #4A7230 stems) for food-icon freshness.

**3. CANONICAL VIEW:** Front-facing **sprig bouquet**: 2–3 stems converging at a bottom cut point, small pointed oval leaves in opposite pairs along each stem, slightly darker stems, no embellishments — the classic "herb bundle" silhouette used for parsley/basil/oregano icons.
