# sage (round 7)

**1. What it reads as now**
- Reads as a generic leafy sprig — basil, mint, or a houseplant stem (Ficus/rubber plant).
- Causes: perfectly oval, smooth-edged symmetric leaves; only 6 leaves on a long bare stem; uniform muted green. No sage-specific texture, no gray-green sage tone emphasis, no branching habit.

**2. Top 3 fixes**
- **Shrink the stem:** sage is a bushy herb — leaves should nearly fill the silhouette, not sit on a long visible stalk. Almost remove the stem entirely.
- **Add sage texture/detail:** lance-shaped leaves with slightly fuzzy/rough edge or a lighter midrib + subtle serration; sage reads "velvety," not glossy-smooth.
- **Shift color to sage's signature gray-green:** desaturated, silvery sage green (#9CAF88 range) with slight tonal variation between leaves, not one flat mid-green.

**3. Canonical view**
- Front-facing bushy clump: 5–7 lanceolate, gray-green leaves radiating from a very short stub of stem, each leaf with a pale midvein, slightly rough/lance silhouette — the "herb bunch" top-down view.
