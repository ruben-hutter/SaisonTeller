# Glossary

Domain terms for SaisonTeller. Definitions describe the business meaning only — no implementation details.

## Produce item

A fruit, vegetable, herb or nut tracked by the calendar. Each has a name in all four app languages (English, German, French, Italian) and a category.

## Harvest month

A month in which a produce item is at its peak local season and is freshly harvested in the reference region. Outside these months the item is either not available or only available from storage.

## Storage month

A month in which a produce item is no longer fresh from the field but is still good from storage (cellar, cold store, drying). An item shown as "from storage" is deliberately distinguished from fresh harvest — it is not the same quality claim.

## Season

One of the four meteorological seasons used to group months: spring (March–May), summer (June–August), autumn (September–November), winter (December–February). Winter wraps around the year boundary.

## Reference region

Switzerland & Central Europe. All harvest and storage months refer to this region; the calendar is a reference, not a guarantee.

## Southern produce

Produce of Mediterranean origin (e.g. citrus, figs) whose seasonality differs from the reference region; highlighted distinctly in the app so users do not mistake it for local harvest.
